    Life on campus

    More than lectures

    College is about learning, friendships and growing into a confident teacher.

    📚

    Learn

    Build strong subject knowledge and the teaching skills you need for the classroom, from lesson planning to classroom management.

    🤝

    Belong

    Meet course mates, join clubs and societies, and make friends who will walk with you long after graduation.

    🎓

    Practise

    Gain real experience through teaching practice, where you try out what you have learned with real pupils.

    🌱

    Grow

    Develop leadership, character and discipline. Your values will matter as much as your grades.
